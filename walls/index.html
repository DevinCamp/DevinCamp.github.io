Up / down alerts

Watches buyers vs sellers on the Coinbase order book within ±0.25% of the price. When one side clearly takes over it marks the chart (▲ buyers, ▼ sellers) and can notify you. It re-arms once the book settles back to balanced. This reads order-book pressure; it is not a guarantee of where price goes.
